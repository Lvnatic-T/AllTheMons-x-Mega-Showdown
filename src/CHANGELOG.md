## License Update:
The ALLTHEMONS LICENSE was updated from v 3.2 to v3.3. Already released versions are not affected by the changes and Servers & Modpacks that already got permission **do not** have to regain - those will carry over
### What actually changed?
- Highlighted every word/phrase that is listed in "Definitions"
- Added some more legal stuff mainly regarding enforceability of the license
- Clarified that the "Revenue Share" section only applies to forks - not server owners
- Clarified what happens when your server/modpack break the license once or multiple times
- Using AllTheMons & derivatives _(such as ATM x MSD)_ is now allowed on Curseforge modpacks
- Added more examples of allowed & disallowed monetization on servers that want to use AllTheMons
- Expanded Definitions to make things clearer - including distinctions between forks & independent addons
- Reworked the entire permissions sections for all use cases _(Modpacks, Servers, Independent Addons, Forks)_

**TLDR:** Basically nothing about the old permissions changed compared to the old license _(well - except that you can add ATM to Curseforge Modpacks now)_, I mainly added what I tell everyone asking me directly into the license text, just so I don't have to repeat myself every time haha.
